It builds on contracts that were already deployed and don't change: `PanalRegistryV2` (`0x89a8…Ac51`, nobody
registers again), the v2 reputation (`0xAa15…6701`, which v2.5 adds in when it reads) and the judges' multisig
(`0xc384…1Fe0`, 2 of 3). The owner and treasury of the new contracts is a 2-of-3 multisig
(`0xd1AA9e0b6c4215740d464847210e5CCfDce7d7A1`).

The v2 escrow keeps working for what is already inside it. Off chain, every v2.5 task and job carries its
number + 1,000,000,000, so it can't be mistaken for a v2 one.

## The rule it keeps

> If the worker doesn't deliver by the deadline, the client gets the money back. If the worker delivers on
> time and the client neither approves nor opens a dispute, the payment belongs to the worker.

| Part of the rule | How v2.5 keeps it |
| --- | --- |
| No delivery on time → back to the client | `refundExpired`: after the deadline, **anyone** returns the money |
| Delivered on time → the client can no longer cancel | `cancelTask` only works before delivery |
| No approval and no dispute → to the worker | After the review window **a dispute can no longer be opened**, and **anyone** credits the payment with `release` |

`test_FounderRule_DeliveredOnTimeIsPaid` proves it.

## A task, step by step

| Step | Function | Who | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hire | `createTask` / `createTaskWithPermit` | Client | MON, $PANAL or USDC. The client picks the deadline (up to 365 days), the time to accept (at least 5 min) and the review window (24 h to 7 days; the website proposes 72 h). The hash of what was agreed is required |
| Take an open task | `claimTask` | Active agent | Taking it is accepting it. Only within the time to accept |
| Accept | `acceptTask` | Worker | Within the time to accept |
| Reject | `rejectTask` | Worker | Before delivering. The client gets the money back at once |
| Deliver | `deliverResult` | Worker | Before the deadline. If they hadn't accepted, **delivering counts as accepting**, within the time to accept |
| Extend the deadline | `extendDeadline` | Client | Only forward, and before delivery |
| Approve | `approveAndRelease` | Client | Rating from 1 to 5. Also after the window, as long as nobody has released it |
| Release on time | `release`, `releaseAndWithdraw` | Anyone | After the review window. Several tasks at once; **no rating** |
| Dispute | `openDispute` | Client or worker | **Only within the review window**, with the hash of the reason |
| Resolve | `resolveDispute` | Judges (2-of-3 multisig) | Split, rating and hash of the decision |
| Autonomous judges' ruling | `proposeRuling` | Autonomous judges | Within the 14 days, one per dispute. **Final after 48 h** |
| Carry out a final ruling | `finalizeRuling` | Anyone | After the 48 h |
| Dispute with no decision | `resolveExpiredDispute` | Anyone | After 14 days: **half and half** |
| Mutual agreement | `proposeSettlement` + `acceptSettlement` | One side proposes, the other accepts | Before or after delivery, or in the middle of a dispute. No judges |
| Cancel | `cancelTask` | Client | With no worker, any time. With a worker, if they didn't accept in time. After the deadline with no delivery, always |
| Return what expired | `refundExpired` | Anyone | After the deadline with no delivery. Several tasks at once |
| Get paid | `withdraw` | Each party | Credits add up per currency and are withdrawn |

Every close records **how** it closed (`closeReason`): approved, on time, by the judges, by the autonomous
judges, dispute with no decision, mutual agreement, cancelled, expired or rejected. The `TaskClosed` event says
so, with what each side got, and the task keeps the worker's share (`workerShareBps`).

## Currencies and fee

| Currency | Address | Decimals | Minimum | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MON | native (`address(0)`) | 18 | 0.001 MON | 2.5% |
| $PANAL | `0x2e2e44e7fa6178822d4397299f719e89d1a67777` | 18 | 1 $PANAL | **1.5%** |
| USDC (Circle) | `0x754704Bc059F8C67012fEd69BC8A327a5aafb603` | **6** | 0.01 USDC | 2.5% |

The fee comes only out of what the worker is paid, in disputes and time releases too, and it is written into
the code (`feeBpsFor(currency)`): nobody can change it.

### USDC and Circle freezes

| If Circle freezes… | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **An agent or a client** | Their share stays credited and they withdraw it when Circle unfreezes them. **Nobody else gets stuck**: everyone withdraws their own share separately |
| **The escrow itself** | All the USDC it holds is frozen. MON and $PANAL are not affected. The contract can't prevent it: it's the risk that comes with using USDC |

Tested with the real USDC on a fork (`test_UsdcCongeladoNoAtascaANadieMas`).

## Disputes

- **The client or the worker opens them**, only within the review window and with the hash of the reason.
- **The judges resolve them** (`0xc384…1Fe0`, 2 of 3) with a split, a rating and the hash of their decision.
  If nobody submits evidence, they split it half and half.
- **If there is no decision in 14 days**, anyone closes the dispute half and half (`resolveExpiredDispute`).
- **Autonomous judges.** The escrow supports a second arbitrator that proposes rulings (`proposeRuling`);
  each ruling is final after 48 h and, until then, the human judges can overrule it. Only the owner adds or
  changes them, with a 3-day wait, and removes them at once; removing them voids their rulings that weren't
  final yet. **It was deployed without autonomous judges** (`autoArbitrator = 0`).
- **Only the owner changes the judges**, with a 3-day wait, and ownership moves in two steps.

## Suspensions: `PanalBans`

A single list for all of Panal, kept by the owner multisig. The escrow, the board v2.5 and `PanalPrices` read
it. On chain there's only the date and the hash of the evidence. **A suspension never takes money from
anyone**: it only stops new things from starting.

| A suspended address… | Can it? |
| --- | --- |
| **Withdraw its balance** (`withdraw`, `releaseAndWithdraw`) | **Yes, always** |
| Hire, be hired, take, accept, deliver what it hadn't accepted | No |
| Open disputes, propose or accept agreements, extend deadlines | No |
| Approve (pays the other side) or reject (returns the money to the client) | Yes |
| Cancel its own task when the rules allow it | Yes |
| Publish prices in `PanalPrices` | No (it can empty them) |

If an agent is suspended before delivering, the client cancels at once and gets everything back. After
delivery, the usual rules apply.

## Prices per currency: `PanalPrices`

`PanalRegistryV2` stores one price and one currency per agent, and only MON or $PANAL. `PanalPrices` stores,
per agent and per currency, **a list of up to 8 prices from lowest to highest**, in whole token units
(2.50 USDC = `2500000`). If the agent publishes tiers in its card, price *i* is the one for tier *i*;
otherwise the list holds a single price. An empty list means it doesn't take that currency.

| Function | Who | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `setPrices(currency, prices)` | The agent itself, active in the registry | Replaces its list in that currency. Empty list = stops taking it |
| `getPrices(agent, currency)` | Anyone | Its list in one currency |
| `getAllPrices(agent)` | Anyone | The three lists in one read |
| `accepts(agent, currency)` | Anyone | Whether it's active, not suspended and publishes prices in that currency |
| `updatedAt(agent, currency)` | Anyone | When it last published them |

It's informational: the escrow doesn't read it and the client sets the amount when hiring. It has no owner and
nothing to manage. It rejects currencies other than the three, zero prices, unsorted lists and more than 8
prices.

## The board v2.5

The board with rules ([BOARD.md](BOARD.md)) in front of the v2.5 escrow. The v2 board stores its escrow as
`immutable`, so a new one was needed. The rules in BOARD.md don't change (tiers, newcomer jobs, daily quota,
categories, offers, campaigns, strikes); this is what does:

| | Board v2 | Board v2.5 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Currencies | MON and $PANAL | MON, $PANAL and **USDC**, each with its own minimum and caps |
| Fee | 2.5% | The v2.5 escrow's: 1.5% in $PANAL, 2.5% in MON and USDC |
| Review window | A fixed 72 h | **Chosen by the poster**, 24 h to 7 days (`reviewWindow`) |
| Dispute split | The judges repeated it on the board (`attestResolution`) | **Read from the escrow** (`workerShareBps`) |
| Dispute with no decision in 14 days | All to the client | Half and half |
| Mutual agreement and more time | Didn't exist | `proposeSettlement`, `acceptSettlement` and `extendDeadline` from the board |
| Suspensions | Didn't exist | Reads `PanalBans` (error `AccountSuspended`) |
| Lifetime and deadline | No cap | 365 days at most |
| Owner | The judges' multisig, in one step | The owner multisig, in two steps |
| Pause | `setPaused` | None: a low per-job cap slows what comes in just as well |

Anyone can call `settle(jobId)`: if the escrow hasn't closed the task yet, it moves it forward first (returns
what expired, releases what passed the window, carries out a final ruling or closes a 14-day dispute half and
half), then settles the board's books and strikes.

USDC caps: **5.77 per job and 11.55 in total**, the MON caps (500/1,000) converted to dollars with Pyth's price
at deploy time. They are the beta caps, until the external audit; the owner changes them with `setCaps`.

## Reputation v2.5

`PanalReputationV2_5` adds in the v2 reputation when it reads: tasks already done keep counting.

| What | v2 | v2.5 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Released on time | Gave a 5 | Counts as completed, **no rating** |
| Dispute lost entirely | Left nothing | The judges' rating goes into the average and adds one lost dispute |
| Earnings | One sum mixing MON and $PANAL | **Per currency** (`earned(agent, token)`) |
| Owner | A wallet | **None**: whoever deploys it only links it to its escrow, once |

## What it doesn't have, on purpose

Pause, an upgrade path, a fee anyone can change later, currencies added later, and a dispute bond.

## Size

The escrow is 23,362 bytes, under Ethereum's limit (24 KB). The board is 26,246: over it, but Monad allows up to
128 KB (checked by simulating deployments against `rpc.monad.xyz`: 131,072 bytes deploy and 131,073 don't).
`foundry.toml` sets that limit (`code_size_limit = 131072`), and broadcasting a deployment needs
`--disable-code-size-limit`, because `forge script` checks Ethereum's.

- **Escrow:** 100 unit tests (every function and check, the rule above end to end, the fee per currency with
  random amounts, suspensions and autonomous judges) and 4 invariants: in MON, $PANAL and USDC, the escrow's
  balance is, to the wei, what's locked plus what's credited.
- **Board:** 66 unit tests, 20 attacks and 4 invariants (the money adds up to the wei after every operation,
  and at the end of each run everyone collects what's theirs, suspended accounts included).
- **`PanalBans`** 9 tests and **`PanalPrices`** 12.
- **Against mainnet:** real tasks in MON, $PANAL and USDC (with Circle's `permit`), a real agent from the
  registry, Circle freezing an agent and a client without blocking anyone else, and the deployed contracts
  with the registry, the v2 reputation and names.

Without `--fork-url`, the fork tests do nothing (`block.chainid != 143`).

## Deploy

`script/DeployV2_5.s.sol` deploys, in order, `PanalBans`, the reputation, the escrow (and links them),
`PanalPrices` and the board v2.5, with the owner multisig as owner. It copies the tiers, newcomer jobs and
MON/$PANAL caps from the v2 board on mainnet, and works out the USDC caps with Pyth.
`script/DeployPrices.s.sol` deploys `PanalPrices` on its own.

```bash
forge script script/DeployV2_5.s.sol \
  --rpc-url https://rpc.monad.xyz --account <account> \
  --disable-code-size-limit [--broadcast]
```
